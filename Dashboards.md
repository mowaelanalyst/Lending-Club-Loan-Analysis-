This dashboard focuses on identifying the primary risk factors and demographic patterns contributing to loan defaults. 
It analyzes default rates across critical dimensions such as debt-to-income ratios, credit grades, annual income brackets, and loan terms. 
By visualizing these metrics, risk management teams can pinpoint high-risk segments—such as lower credit tiers and high debt ratios—allowing financial institutions to tighten underwriting criteria and mitigate potential credit losses effectively
